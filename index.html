About Pito Galera

Pito Galera is a stunning coastal destination located in Barangay Pinamucan, Batangas City. Named after the famous Puerto Galera for its similarly pristine waters, this hidden gem offers visitors a peaceful retreat surrounded by natural beauty.

With its calm waters and scenic views, Pito Galera is ideal for swimming, photography, or simply relaxing by the sea. The area maintains its natural charm while being easily accessible from Batangas City proper.

Whether you're looking for a day trip or a weekend getaway, Pito Galera provides the perfect setting to unwind and reconnect with nature away from the crowds of more commercial destinations.

How to Get to Pito Galera

Pito Galera is conveniently located in Barangay Pinamucan Proper, just 20-25 minutes from Batangas City proper.

By Public Transportation:
From Batangas Grand Terminal, take a jeepney or tricycle bound for Pinamucan. Ask the driver to drop you off at Pito Galera.

By Private Vehicle:
Take the Diversion Road and follow signs leading to Pinamucan. The coastal area is easily accessible with ample parking space available.

Best Time to Visit:
The dry season from November to May offers the best weather conditions for beach activities and photography.
